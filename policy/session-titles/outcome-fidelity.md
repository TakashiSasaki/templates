# Describe the observed session outcome in the title

A finalized conversation-session title must describe the session's actual result rather than merely restating its initial objective. Include enough of the principal object or subsystem, the principal action or result, and the established terminal state to distinguish the session from nearby work. Do not imply completion, merge, deployment, review completion, handoff readiness, or any other state that has not been established by the applicable evidence and completion semantics.

Prefer concrete outcome terms such as implementation, remediation, validation, audit, deployment, merge, review handoff, design, investigation, or their context-appropriate equivalents. Avoid generic titles that identify only the existence of work. A status prefix or other compact state marker may be used when the interaction convention supports it, but it must encode the established terminal state rather than the topic alone.
