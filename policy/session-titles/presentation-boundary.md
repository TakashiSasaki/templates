# Keep session-title presentation separate from work authority

Treat a conversation-session title as presentation and navigation metadata, not as repository state, validation evidence, review evidence, merge authorization, or completion authority. When the active interaction surface supports an authorized mechanism for persisting the title, that mechanism may be used; otherwise provide the same title as a terminal title proposal. The absence or failure of a title-persistence mechanism must not change the established work state, reopen completed work, block an otherwise valid handoff or completion boundary, or justify a false completion claim.
